stand for object-oriented programming
introduced in 1960s with the language Simula
later used in Smalltalk, C++, Java, C#, Python, and many other languages

in oop objects are the building blocks of program
objects are reprent anything from real life or abstract ideas

each object contans data and behavior
abstraction, encapsulation, inheritance, and polymorphism are the four main principles of oop
